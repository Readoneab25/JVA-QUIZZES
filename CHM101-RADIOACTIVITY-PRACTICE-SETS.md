# CHM 101 — Radioactivity (Dr. Rajee) — Practice Sets 01–04

Four standalone, source-grounded, 30-question, four-option MCQ quizzes (120 total) covering **only the 11 pages provided** in `CHM101 (Radioactivity) – Dr. Rajee.pdf`. The upload ends at the beginning of Module 4, after a short statement of nuclear-equation balancing rules and a particle-symbol table. Later modules listed in the table of contents are **not present**; no questions have been invented about their unseen content.

| Set | Content | Questions | Time |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| 01 | Overview, discovery history, natural vs induced radioactivity, nuclear vs chemical processes | 30 | 20 min |
| 02 | Nucleus, nuclides, nucleon forces, N/Z ratio, stability, binding energy | 30 | 20 min |
| 03 | Alpha, beta-minus, beta-plus, electron capture, gamma, penetration, shielding, equation rules | 30 | 20 min |
| 04 | Applied nuclear notation, mass-defect arithmetic, balanced decay calculations, radiation protection | 30 | 20 min |

All 120 stems are unique. Where a concept recurs, the task differs: factual recall vs interpretation or calculation. Students do not receive hints or the answer key before submission.
